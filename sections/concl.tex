\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:concl}

In this work, we introduce a novel fault model that captures the nature of correlated failures in decentralized environments.
Using this model, we run simulations to demonstrate surprising shortcomings in existing decentralized storage solutions in providing durability.
Based on the simulation insights, we propose a new storage protocol, \sys.
\sys leverages system-wide placement groups to tolerate arbitrary correlated failures.
It also applies rateless erasure codes and confidential sampling to improve practicality in large deployments.
We believe our approach offers a promising direction for achieving the ``holy-grail'' of decentralized storage --- provably durability under adversarial network conditions.
