\section{Background}
\label{sec:bg}

\begin{outline}
Systems that do not repair at all: Filecoin, Sia.
Push the repair burden to storage nodes.
Increase centralization.

Systems that ``implicitly repair'' by encouraging replication: EthStorage, Arweave.
Usually not deploy erasure codes; low space efficiency.
Rely on storage nodes to discover profitable storing opportunities; either full replica or costly scanning.

Systems that orchestrate dissemination and repairing: Storj, Swarm.
\xxx{Shortage.}
\end{outline}
