\section{Future Work}

Building upon the promising preliminary results, we intend to finalize this work as outlined.
We will develop a formal theoretical framework for \dht. This will begin with a probabilistic definition of effective overall utilization, followed by the formulation of probabilistic utilization in terms of system parameters and the consistent hashing scheme.
Subsequently, we will enhance our simulation efforts with discrete event simulations. These simulations will model \dht and the Kademlia baseline over a defined time period, collecting various statistics, including the overhead introduced by 2RC and the efficacy of dynamically adjusting node classes. We will also simulate Kademlia with the virtual node approach to provide a more comprehensive comparison.
Finally, we will implement \dht and deploy it in a real-world setting. This will involve setting up DSNs on cloud infrastructure such as AWS across various global regions to measure \dht's actual data storage and retrieval performance, comparing it against Kademlia. We anticipate a noticeable but acceptable performance overhead associated with \dht.
