\section{Conclusion}
In this work the durability problem of DSNs that is caused by the uniform sampling of DHT protocols is revealed.
To address it, we propose \dht, a novel DHT that can differentiate the discoverability of nodes based on their storage capacities.
The differentiated discoverability enables \dht to effectively balance the availability capacities of storage nodes and avoid overwhelming nodes, and eventually improves the durability.
\dht shows a promising research direction of DHT, and we will continue implement it and evaluate its effectiveness in the real world.
