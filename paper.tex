\section{Conclusion}
Given the increasing importance of DSNs that manage heterogeneous storage, \dht offers the potential to significantly bolster their security and enhance user experience, paving the way for broader adoption, particularly in mission-critical applications.
Moreover, while this work centers on storage heterogeneity, we believe the underlying principles of \dht are adaptable and can be extended to address various forms of heterogeneity within decentralized systems.
\dht presents a promising research trajectory for DHTs, and we will continue to implement and rigorously evaluate its effectiveness in the future.
